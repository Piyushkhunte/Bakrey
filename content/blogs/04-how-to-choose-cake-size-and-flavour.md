# How to Choose the Right Cake Size and Flavour for Your Celebration

**Choose a cake by starting with guest count, then confirm flavour, dietary needs, and serving time with the bakery.** This approach is more reliable than selecting a cake only because it looks good in a photo.

## Choose size from the way you will serve it

Tell the bakery how many people will actually eat cake. If it is the main dessert, you may want a more generous serving. If it follows a full meal with several desserts, smaller slices may be enough. Children’s parties, office celebrations, and family events can all have different serving patterns.

The bakery can translate your guest count into a practical cake recommendation. Mention whether you want extra slices for family members or leftovers for the next day.

## Pick a flavour your guests will enjoy

Start with a familiar flavour when you do not know everyone’s preference. A familiar option is usually the easiest choice for a mixed group. If the celebration is for one person, their favourite flavour can be the better priority.

Give the bakery a first choice and a backup. Ask what is best suited to the selected design, and whether the cake should be refrigerated. This turns a vague request into a decision you can act on.

## Do not leave dietary requirements to the end

Tell the bakery about eggless requirements, allergies, and ingredients to avoid before you confirm the order. Request a direct answer about ingredients and possible cross-contact. Never treat a general product description as an allergy guarantee.

This is especially important if the cake will be shared by children or guests with a medically necessary dietary restriction.

## Match the design to the occasion

For an elegant celebration, a simple message and a colour palette may be enough. For a child’s birthday, a theme or favourite character may matter more. Send one or two clear reference images rather than a large set of conflicting designs.

The most useful design brief includes the recipient’s name, exact message, event date, preferred colours, and any elements to avoid. Check spelling carefully before the bakery begins preparation.

## Plan pickup or delivery carefully

Confirm the final time the cake is needed, then arrange pickup or delivery with a buffer. Keep the cake level while travelling, follow storage instructions, and take it out at the recommended time before serving.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know what cake size to order?

Share the guest count and whether cake is the main dessert. The bakery can recommend an appropriate size based on its own portion guidance.

### What is the safest flavour for a mixed group?

A familiar flavour is usually the simplest choice, but confirm what is currently available and consider any dietary requirements first.

### What should I include in a custom cake request?

Include date, guest count, flavour, dietary needs, budget, exact message, design reference, and pickup or delivery time.
