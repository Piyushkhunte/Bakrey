# How to Order a Birthday Cake in Pune: A Simple Checklist

**To order a birthday cake successfully, share the date, number of guests, flavour, dietary requirements, message, and pickup or delivery time first.** These details let a bakery confirm the right size and whether the order can be prepared on time.

## 1. Start with the celebration date and time

Tell the bakery when the cake will be cut, not only the event date. A cake required at lunchtime needs a different pickup or delivery plan from one needed in the evening. Ordering early is particularly useful for weekends, holidays, detailed decorations, and larger guest lists.

When you contact Piyush's Bakery, include your preferred pickup or delivery time in the first message. Ask the team to confirm the final handover time rather than assuming it.

## 2. Estimate how many people you are serving

Guest count is the most practical starting point for choosing a cake size. Let the bakery know whether cake is the main dessert or one part of a larger spread. If children and adults are both attending, mention that too.

You do not need to calculate every slice yourself. A bakery can recommend a suitable size once it knows the guest count and the type of celebration.

## 3. Choose flavour and dietary requirements

Give the bakery one preferred flavour and one backup. Also state any essential requirement clearly, including eggless preparation, allergies, or ingredients to avoid. Never assume a cake is suitable for an allergy; ask the bakery about ingredients and possible cross-contact before ordering.

If the design is more important than a particular flavour, share that as well. A clear priority helps the team make a useful recommendation.

## 4. Confirm the cake message and design

Write the cake message exactly as it should appear, including spelling and punctuation. For a custom design, send one or two reference images and specify the colours or theme you like. Keep the request focused: a birthday message, favourite colour, hobby, or simple theme is usually more helpful than a large collection of unrelated examples.

Before finalising, confirm whether decorations are edible and whether a topper, candles, or a knife are included.

## 5. Confirm price, payment, collection, and storage

Ask for the final price, advance-payment requirement, pickup address, delivery coverage, and collection instructions. If delivery is requested, provide a reachable contact number and a clear landmark. Once you receive the cake, ask how long it can stay unrefrigerated and when it should be served.

## Frequently asked questions

### How early should I order a birthday cake?

Order as early as you can, especially for a weekend, a large cake, or a custom design. The bakery should confirm its exact lead time.

### What details does a bakery need for a custom cake?

Provide the date, guest count, flavour, dietary needs, message, reference image or theme, budget, and pickup or delivery time.

### Can I request an eggless birthday cake?

Ask when ordering and get confirmation in writing. Also disclose allergies, because eggless does not automatically mean free from all allergens or cross-contact.
